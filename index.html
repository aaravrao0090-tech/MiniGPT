What you're learning

Training: MiniGPT counts which character tends to follow each short context in your examples.

Generation: It starts with your prompt, samples a likely next character, and repeats the process.

Experiment: Add more varied text, retrain, and compare how the output changes. The context length controls how much recent text the model considers.
